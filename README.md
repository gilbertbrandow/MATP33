# MATP33 - Group and Ring Theory

MATP33 Group and Ring Theory, 7.5 credits, is an alternative-compulsory course at advanced level for a Master of Science degree in mathematics. It can also be taken as a stand-alone course. The course is given at half study pace during the first half of the autumn semester. The language of instruction is English.

## Course content

The course covers:

- Groups: permutation groups, Burnside's lemma with applications to Polya enumeration, Sylow's theorems, symmetric and alternating groups, and the structure of finitely generated Abelian groups
- Rings and modules: Noetherian and Artinian rings and modules, the Wedderburn-Artin theorem, and finitely generated modules over a principal ideal domain, with applications to the Jordan normal form of matrices
- Linear algebra: multilinear mappings and tensor products

## Examination

The examination consists of:

- A written examination worth 5 credits
- An oral examination worth 2.5 credits
- The oral examination may only be taken after passing the written examination

Information and instructions for the oral examinations will be posted on Canvas. The available material does not specify the grading scale, permitted aids, or any mandatory participation requirements.
